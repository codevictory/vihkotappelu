Rules
  • 32x32 grid, turn based: on your turn every unit may move once and attack once (if a target is in range), then the other side goes.
  • Click a unit of the active side, then a green square to move and a red square to attack. Spent units are dimmed — press End turn to pass the rest.
  • Damage = attacker's ATK minus target's DEF (minimum 1).
  • Archers cannot shoot adjacent squares (min range 2).
  • Units block movement. Destroying every enemy unit wins the battle.
  • Forests, mountains and houses are obstacles: units cannot move onto or through them.

Hotseat mode: you command both sides, alternating turns. Game state lives on the server per room, so online duels against a human can be added on top of the same API.
